DATA MODEL
- Housemate: id, name, avatar color
- Chore: id, title, optional description, assignee (housemate), status ("To-do" | "In progress" | "Completed"), due date, created date, completed date (set automatically when status changes to Completed, cleared if moved back)

SCREENS
1. Chores list (default screen)
   - "Add chore" button opens a modal with title, description, assignee dropdown, and due date picker
   - Each chore is a card showing title, assignee avatar + name, due date, and a status pill
   - Status can be changed in one click from the card (dropdown or segmented control)
   - Filter by status and by housemate; sort by due date
   - Overdue chores (past due, not completed) are visually highlighted
   - Edit and delete chores (confirm before delete)
2. Calendar
   - Month view with a toggle to week view
   - Show each chore on its due date, color-coded by assignee
   - Also show a distinct marker (e.g., checkmark) on the date a chore was completed
   - Clicking a day shows that day's chores in a side panel; clicking a chore opens its edit modal
   - Legend explaining colors and markers
3. Housemates settings
   - Add, rename, and remove housemates; pick an avatar color

NAVIGATION
- Top nav on desktop, bottom tab bar on mobile, switching between List, Calendar, and Housemates

VISUAL DESIGN
- Friendly, calm, and uncluttered, similar in feel to Todoist or Notion Calendar
- Soft neutral background, white rounded cards with subtle shadows, generous spacing
- One accent color (e.g., teal) plus status colors: gray = To-do, amber = In progress, green = Completed
- Clean sans-serif font (Inter), clear type hierarchy
- Smooth, subtle transitions when status changes or modals open
- Fully responsive and usable on a phone

QUALITY DETAILS
- Friendly empty states ("No chores yet — add your first one")
- Form validation (title and assignee required)
- Accessible: keyboard navigable, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast
